Story

Tuna has been fished in the Mediterranean for over 2,000 years. Japanese Bluefin can sell for over $3 million at Tokyo's Tsukiji fish market. The global tuna industry exceeds $40 billion annually, making it one of the most commercially important fish species.

Tuna is a wild-caught marine fish. Aquaculture for Bluefin exists but is limited. This tab is more relevant for plant-based ingredients.

TUNA PASTA

Rich, creamy, and spicy tuna pasta

Info
Serves 4
Time25 Mins
Per Serving
Energy638 kCal
Carb74.0g
Protein30.2g
Fat21.3g

Ingredients

Tagliatelle320g3 cups
Tuna Flakes170g1 can
Olive Oil45ml3 tbsp
Garlic, minced5 cloves
Onion, diced1 medium
Thyme2 sprigs
Tarragon1 tsp
Rosemary1 sprig
Chilli Flakes1 pinch
Parsley, chopped2 tbsp
White Wine125ml1/2 cup
Tomato Purée375ml1 1/2 cups
Sugar12g1 tbsp
Parmigiano Reggiano75g1 cup
Lemon, squeezed1/2 small
Salt2 tbsp
Pepper, freshly ground1 tbsp

Instructions

1

Add 2 tbsp olive oil and tuna flakes in a sauce pan and cook on medium heat until golden brown.

2

Remove tuna from the heat and place on a dish or in a container.

3

Add 1 tbsp olive oil, onion, garlic, thyme, tarragon, rosemary, and chilli flakes in the sauce pan. Cook until the onions have softened.

4

Deglaze with white wine, let the alcohol cook out.

5

Add tomato purée, salt, sugar, and pepper. Cook for 15 minutes and add parmigiano reggiano. Meanwhile, bring 8 cups of water to a boil in a pot and add 1 tbsp salt. Add tagliatelle to water and cook most of the way.

6

Add tuna from earlier to the sauce pan, mix, and cook for 1 minute.

7

Taste and adjust for salt and pepper.

8

Add the tagliatelle and some of the starchy water to the sauté pan, mix, and let the tagliatelle finish cooking in the sauce. Grate some parmigiano reggiano on top while cooking.

5

Add the pasta to the sauce, mix and serve with parmesan cheese.

Note

For a spicier kick, double the chilli flakes or add a dash of hot sauce when adding the white wine. Ensure the tuna is well-drained before adding it to the pan for the best sear.

Variations

  • Gluten-Free: Substitute standard tagliatelle with your favorite gluten-free pasta alternative.
  • Vegetarian: Omit the tuna and add capers and olives for a puttanesca-style dish.

CLASSIC ECLAIR

Crisp choux pastry filled with rich vanilla pastry cream

Info
Serves 12
Time2 Hours

Ingredients

For the Choux Pastry

Water125ml
Butter50g
Plain Flour75g
Eggs2 large
Salt1 pinch

For the Pastry Cream

Whole Milk250ml
Egg Yolks3 large
Sugar50g
Cornstarch20g
Vanilla Extract1 tsp

For the Glaze

Dark Chocolate100g
Heavy Cream50ml

Instructions

Making the Pastry Cream

1

In a bowl, whisk together egg yolks, sugar, and cornstarch until pale and smooth.

2

Heat milk in a saucepan until simmering. Gradually pour hot milk into the egg mixture while whisking constantly.

3

Return the mixture to the saucepan and cook over medium heat, whisking constantly until thickened. Stir in vanilla, transfer to a bowl, cover with plastic wrap touching the surface, and chill.

Making the Choux Pastry

1

Preheat oven to 200°C. In a saucepan, bring water, butter, and salt to a boil.

2

Remove from heat and stir in flour all at once. Return to heat and cook, stirring vigorously, until mixture forms a ball and pulls away from sides of pan.

3

Let cool slightly. Beat in eggs one at a time, ensuring each is fully incorporated before adding the next. The dough should be glossy and pipeable.

Baking and Assembly

1

Pipe the choux pastry into 4-inch logs on a lined baking sheet. Bake for 20-25 minutes until puffed and golden brown. Let cool completely.

2

Poke holes in the ends of the eclairs and pipe the chilled pastry cream inside.

3

Melt chocolate and cream together. Dip the tops of the filled eclairs into the glaze and let set.

Recipe Note

Ensure your eggs for the choux pastry are at room temperature. When beating the eggs into the dough, the exact number may vary slightly depending on the flour and egg size; stop when the dough is glossy and slowly falls from the spatula in a "V" shape.
